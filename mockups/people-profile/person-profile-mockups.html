Somebody else's profile, three ways

The screen you reach from View Profile on the Match tab still uses the old flat list style: hairline dividers straight on the page, a grey bio box, 11px type. The rest of the app moved to white lifted cards on the blue-grey page on 14 September. Below: how it looks today, then three ways to redo it. All three use the app's real theme colours, so any of them, or a mix, is a straight rebuild.

Today

What's wrong

  • Sections sit directly on the page with hairline dividers, the pre-September look.
  • The bio is a grey box on the page. The rule since 14 Sep: anything that isn't the page is a white card.
  • Text is 11 to 12px everywhere, so the Training and About rows feel like fine print.
  • The centered avatar and name leave big empty margins on either side.
  • Interests are three uniform squares per row, so long words get cut.

Direction A

Cards

The same content, the same order, moved onto the app's white cards. The least risky option and the one that matches your own Profile tab most closely.

  • Photo on the left, name and house on the right, bio underneath, all in one identity card.
  • Each section is its own lifted card. No grey boxes anywhere.
  • Body text goes up to 13px, labels stay small caps.
  • Interests become chips that fit their word. Shared ones keep the gold tick.
  • Follow and Message stay pinned to the bottom.
Effort: small. Two hours of class changes.

Direction B

Photo first

Their photo becomes the top of the screen, the way a dating or team app does it. The name sits on the photo, and you can swipe through their other photos right there.

  • Back button floats on the photo. No separate title bar.
  • Follow and Message move up, right under the photo, so they are the first thing after the face. No bar pinned to the bottom.
  • Everything else is the same white cards as A.
  • Somebody with no photo gets a soft blue-grey block with their initials instead, like the Match card today.
Effort: medium. The most "app-like" of the three.

Direction C

At a glance

Built around the question the tap was asking: should I train with this person? The answer is on the first screen, not in a list.

  • Identity card with a thin Dunster red and gold bar, taken from the house data the Gyms tab already has.
  • Follow and Message live inside the identity card, so you can act without scrolling.
  • "Why you match" is a crimson-tinted card. It is the one loud thing on the page.
  • Training is four tiles plus a real week strip, so you see which days without tapping a chevron.
  • Interests, About, Photos as in A.
Effort: medium. The biggest change in how it reads.
