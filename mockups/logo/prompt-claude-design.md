# Brief to paste into Claude Design — the UNIsport mark

Rule 8: whatever comes back is a MOCKUP. The shapes are the deliverable; its
colours get re-mapped onto `l-*` tokens when the winner is rebuilt natively as
an SVG component. Drop the export in this folder.

Written 2026-08-23, when the app icon was still a blank navy square
(`public/icons/icon-512.png`) and the only mark was the wordmark.

---

Design a logo mark for **UNIsport**.

**What it is.** A university fitness app. Students find every gym on campus,
get matched with a training partner who actually fits them, log the session
together, and see where they rank. It ships white-label: each university runs
it in its own colours. There is also a gated "Varsity Mode" for official
teams — that side already has its own mark (a shield behind crossed oars) and
is NOT what you are designing.

**The line it has to carry:** *Never train alone.*

**The idea to start from:** build it out of the letter **U**. I want a symbol,
not a wordmark — something that can stand alone as an app icon.

Three directions worth exploring, plus anything better you see:
1. **Two of something.** A U whose two stems read as two people standing
   together, joined at the base. The product is about not training alone; the
   mark could say that before a word is read.
2. **A U that lifts.** A U with weight plates capping its stems — a barbell
   seen head-on. Instant, but it is also the most crowded corner of the
   category, so it has to be drawn better than the obvious version.
3. **A U that runs.** Concentric U strokes — the bend of a running track, or a
   lane. Sport without naming one sport.

**Hard constraints.**
- **Flat.** Solid shapes or single-weight strokes. No gradients, no bevels, no
  3D, no shadows, no photographic texture.
- **Two colours at most**, and one of them may be the accent. Palette: ground
  `#0a0a0a`, off-white `#f5f5f5`, accent blue `#4a9eff`. It must also survive
  as a single flat colour (one-colour version required).
- **Legible at 20px.** It becomes a favicon and a phone home-screen icon. If a
  detail disappears at 20px, it should not be there at 100px either.
- **It must not carry any university's identity.** No crest, no shield, no
  ivy, no laurel, no specific school colour. This mark will sit on a page
  beside Harvard crimson, Yale blue and Princeton orange without clashing with
  any of them. That is the whole business model.
- **No oars, no boats.** Those belong to Varsity Mode's mark.
- Works on a near-black ground first (that is the product's page), and on white
  second.

**What to give me.**
1. **Six to eight distinct marks** on one dark board. Distinct means different
   *ideas*, not the same shape at eight stroke weights.
2. Each one shown three times: large (~96px), small (24px), and on a rounded
   app-icon tile.
3. Under each, one line: what it is meant to read as.
4. Then take your **strongest one** and show a lockup with the wordmark, which
   is set in Instrument Serif italic and reads `UNI` in off-white and `sport`
   in the accent blue — horizontal (mark left, wordmark right) and stacked
   (mark above, wordmark below).
5. Include the one-colour version of that strongest one.

Do not redesign the wordmark. The mark is the job.
