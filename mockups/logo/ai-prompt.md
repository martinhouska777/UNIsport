# UNIsport logo — prompt for an image AI (10 previews)

Paste everything below the line into Midjourney, ChatGPT / DALL·E, Ideogram or similar.
Ideogram and ChatGPT handle the word "UNIsport" best; Midjourney will misspell it, so
for Midjourney delete the two sentences about the wordmark and ask for the symbol only.

---

Design 10 different logo concepts for **UNIsport**, a university fitness app, and show them
as one clean grid of 10 previews (2 rows of 5), each on a plain white square, numbered 1 to 10.

**What the brand is.** UNIsport is the app students open so they never train alone again.
Its promise is "Your campus. Your gym. Your people." It shows every gym on campus, matches you
with other students at your university (verified by their university email) who train the way
you do, lets you plan the session together in the chat, and log it together afterwards. It was
built by a college rower who kept arriving at the gym with nobody to train with.

**Brand values, in order of importance.**
1. Meeting new people. Sport is the excuse, the friend is the point. This is the value every
   logo must carry.
2. Belonging to a campus. University, not a commercial gym chain. Collegiate, a little classic.
3. Doing it together. Two people, not a crowd and not a lone hero.
4. Trust. Real students, real names, verified. Calm and solid, never loud.
5. Effort that is shared. Fitness is present but secondary: no flexing muscles, no flames, no
   lightning bolts, no running silhouettes.

**Visual rules.**
- Flat vector logo marks, geometric or monoline, clean and minimal. Think of a mark that
  works as a 16 px app icon and embroidered on a sweatshirt.
- Colours: a deep navy (#2f3b52), an electric blue (#1f32c1), and white only. Two colours
  maximum per mark. Also render each in a single colour where it helps.
- No gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no swooshes, no shields with laurels, no generic dumbbell
  or heart-rate icons, no stock-style people illustrations, no emoji.
- The letter U is the natural anchor for the brand; many concepts can be built from it, but
  not all ten have to be.
- Show the symbol alone. Where a wordmark helps, add the word "UNIsport" once, spelled exactly
  like that, in a classic italic serif, with "sport" in the electric blue. No other text.

**The 10 concepts to draw, one per tile.** Concepts 1 and 8 are the two the founder already
likes best; give those two the most care, and let concepts 2 to 7 and 9 to 10 be honest
alternatives, not variations of them.
1. **Two meet, one letter.** Two rounded strokes, one navy and one blue, that come down from
   opposite sides and join at the bottom to form a U.
2. **Two heads, one U.** Two abstract head-and-shoulder shapes side by side whose combined
   outline reads as a U.
3. **The plan.** Two speech bubbles overlapping; the shared space between them forms a U.
4. **High five.** Two open hands meeting, reduced to a few geometric strokes, with a U in the
   space between them.
5. **Matched.** Two U shapes interlocked like chain links, one navy and one blue.
6. **Same place, same time.** Two location pins leaning toward each other so their heads touch,
   forming a single joined shape.
7. **Two chairs, one bench.** A single flat bench or bar with two identical rounded shapes sitting
   on it, side by side.
8. **The gate.** A campus archway or doorway, and two small figures standing in it together.
9. **Half and half.** A ring made of two halves in the two colours, with a small gap where they
   meet at the top, like two people about to shake hands.
10. **Fist bump.** Two rounded blocks pushing toward each other; the negative space between them
    is a U.

Render all ten with the same line weight, the same corner radius, and the same size, so they can
be compared fairly. Label each tile with its number only.
