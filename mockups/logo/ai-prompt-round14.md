# UNIsport logo — round 14 prompt for an image AI

Written 2026-09-22 after the owner's reading of the mark: "the letter i looks like a
silhouette of a body and a head, and the U is two i's connected at the bottom." Round 13
fixed the seam (straight, 50/50). This prompt asks an image model for new marks that keep
that reading and add the two things the current mark does not say: university and sport.

Run through ChatCut `submit_image` (gpt-image-2.5-flare, 1:1, 1K, high) with count 2, so two
different 3x3 sheets come back — 18 concepts. The result files land in
`mockups/logo/ai-round14/`.

---

Design 9 logo concepts for **UNIsport** and show them as one clean 3 x 3 grid, each concept on
its own plain white square tile with generous margin, numbered 1 to 9 in small grey type in the
corner. No other text anywhere. No wordmark.

**What the brand is.** UNIsport is the app university students open so they never train alone
again. It matches you with other students at your own university who train the way you do, you
plan the session together in the chat, and you log it together afterwards. Sport is the excuse,
the friend is the point.

**The idea every concept must carry.** Two people meeting. In the current logo each person is
drawn like a lowercase letter i — a round dot for the head, a rounded vertical stroke for the
body — and the two bodies curve toward each other and join at the bottom to make the letter U.
One person is deep navy (#2f3b52), the other electric blue (#1f32c1). Keep that figure language:
head as a dot, body as a rounded monoline stroke, no faces, no arms unless the concept needs
them, no gender, no running poses.

**What to add.** Somewhere in each concept, quietly, either university or sport — never both,
never loud. University can be an arch, a doorway, a lintel, a pennant, a varsity patch shape.
Sport can be a bar the two figures hold, a bench they sit on, a track lane, a ball, a finish
line. Never a dumbbell icon, never a heart-rate line, never flames, lightning, wings, laurels,
shields or muscles.

**Visual rules.** Flat vector, geometric monoline, identical stroke weight and corner radius in
all nine tiles. Two colours plus white only. No gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture, no
outlines around shapes. Every mark must survive as a 16 px app icon and as embroidery on a
sweatshirt, so nothing thinner than the stroke, nothing smaller than the head dot.

**The 9 concepts, one per tile.**
1. **Two i's, one U.** The current mark, drawn as cleanly as possible: two figures whose bodies
   join at the bottom into a U, the seam a straight vertical line dead centre.
2. **In the doorway.** The same two figures standing inside a simple campus arch; the arch and
   the U share the same stroke.
3. **Under one lintel.** The two figures with a single flat bar resting across the tops of
   their heads, like a gate or the bar of a rack.
4. **Holding the bar.** Two figures with the bottom curve of the U replaced by a straight bar
   they both hold, small round plates at the ends inside the U's width.
5. **On the bench.** Two dots (heads) above a single long rounded bench, the bench's ends
   curving up into the two bodies.
6. **The pennant.** A triangular college pennant whose hoist end is the U made of two figures.
7. **The patch.** The two-figure U inside a rounded varsity-letter patch shape, stitched edge
   suggested by a single inner line.
8. **The lane.** Two figures side by side on one track lane drawn as two long parallel strokes
   curving up into their bodies.
9. **Same finish.** Two figures whose bodies join not at the bottom but at a single point, a
   finish-line dot, so the shape reads as a V that is also a U.

Give the tiles the same size, the same visual weight, and a calm, classic, trustworthy feel —
collegiate, not commercial gym.
