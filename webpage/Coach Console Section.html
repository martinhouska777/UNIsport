The Coach’s Console.

Students use the app. Coaches run it. Everything the varsity story above depends on — the plan, the boats, the notes — is built here and published to the whole squad at once. These are real screens from the console as it works today.

1 · Build

Two taps per session.

Pick the type — water, erg, weights. Pick the intensity. Then tap one of the five workouts this squad actually uses and the session fills itself in. A full training week takes minutes, not an evening.

9:415G
2 · Plan

Publish the week once.

The season is one block — fourteen weeks, counting down to the race. Water, erg and weights for every AM and PM, colour-coded by intensity. Publish, and it’s on every athlete’s phone. No more screenshots of a spreadsheet in the group chat.

9:415G
3 · Lineup

Publish the boats.

Seat by seat, cox to bow — port in red , starboard in green , straight from each rower’s profile. One tap and the lineup is on every phone the night before, not shouted across the dock.

9:415G
4 · Notes

Keep every athlete on track.

One short technical note per athlete. It sits on their Home — under their race countdown — until it’s fixed. Everyone else sees a green “Good job”. Forty athletes, one glance, no noise.

Everything the console publishes is the story you just scrolled — the plan on their Home, their name in the boat, the note under their race.

A student who signs up brings one user. A coach who adopts brings the whole squad. That’s why the console exists — and why it’s already built.
